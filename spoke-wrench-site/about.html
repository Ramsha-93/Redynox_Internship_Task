Our story

From a garage on Cedar Lane

Spoke & Wrench started in 2016 as two bike racks and a workbench in a rented garage. The idea was simple: fix bikes properly, charge fairly, and never make anyone feel silly for not knowing what a derailleur is.

Ten years later we're still on Cedar Lane, just with a proper storefront and a lot more tools. The bikes have changed — more electric, more cargo bikes doing school runs — but the job hasn't. Something's making a noise it shouldn't, and we figure out why.

We also rent bikes to anyone who wants to explore Northgate without a car. Half our regulars started as one-day rental customers who got hooked on riding.
